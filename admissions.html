Admissions

Admission information

Admission to STARONIC is based on a transparent and family-friendly process designed to ensure each learner is well placed to thrive within our academic and values-based environment.

Requirements

Parents or guardians are invited to provide basic student information, previous academic records where applicable, and any documents required by the school for placement and onboarding.

How to apply

Parents are encouraged to contact the school to begin the admission process and receive details about available spaces, timelines, and required next steps.
