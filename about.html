About us

Our history

STARONIC was established to provide a learning environment where students can develop academically, socially, and morally. Since its beginning, the school has grown through dedication, strong values, and a commitment to nurturing every child’s potential.

Vision

TO SEE EVERY CHILD IDENTIFY, DEVELOP AND USE THEIR UNIQUE TALENTS TO FULFIL THEIR GOD-GIVEN DESTINY. At STARONIC, we believe every learner is created with purpose, and our role is to nurture the strengths, character, and confidence needed to help them become all that God has called them to be.

Mission

Staronic Educational Complex is established with the mission R.P. To Raise and train up children with the mind of Christ our Lord. To Produce faithful and loyal citizens and leaders who shall be at the service of the community and to help build a nation with Godly values. We are committed to shaping young people who are spiritually grounded, academically capable, socially responsible, and ready to serve their families, communities, and nation with wisdom and integrity.

Core value

  • Respect – Treating others with kindness, fairness, and courtesy.
  • Responsibility – Being accountable for one’s actions and duties.
  • Honesty – Telling the truth and acting with integrity.
  • Kindness – Showing compassion, empathy, and support for others.
  • Perseverance – Trying hard and not giving up, even when faced with challenges.
  • Teamwork – Working well with others and valuing collaboration.
  • Excellence – Striving to do one’s best in all areas of school life.
  • Courage – Being brave in facing new challenges, trying hard and not giving up.

These values guide how we teach, how we relate to one another, and how we help our learners grow into confident, compassionate, and capable young people.
