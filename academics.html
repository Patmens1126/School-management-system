Academics

Classes offered

STARONIC provides a structured academic pathway that supports learners from foundational skills through to higher-level learning outcomes. Classes are designed to encourage both mastery of core subjects and strong personal development.

Departments and subjects

Our curriculum includes literacy, numeracy, science, technology, social studies, arts, and other core learning areas. Each subject is taught with an emphasis on understanding, confidence, and practical application.

Academic approach

We combine strong classroom teaching with guided practice, active participation, and continuous learner support. Our academic model helps students become independent thinkers, confident communicators, and responsible learners.
